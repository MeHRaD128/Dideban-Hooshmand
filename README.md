# Dideban Hooshmand

**Dideban Hooshmand** is a smart financial market analysis platform designed to provide market signals, analytical insights, and useful information for stock markets and other financial markets.

The project aims to help traders and investors better understand market conditions by combining financial data, technical analysis, market indicators, and intelligent signal generation.

## Features

* 📊 Financial market analysis
* 📈 Stock market signals
* 💹 Technical indicators and market trends
* 🔔 Trading and market alerts
* 📋 Watchlists and market monitoring
* 🤖 Intelligent analysis and signal generation
* 📉 Price and market data visualization
* 🌐 Support for multiple financial markets
* 📱 Modern and user-friendly interface

## Vision

The goal of Dideban Hooshmand is to build a reliable and intelligent platform that makes financial market data and analysis easier to understand and more accessible to traders and investors.

> **Dideban Hooshmand does not guarantee financial returns or predict the market with certainty. Market signals and analysis are provided for informational and analytical purposes only.**

More features, analytical tools, market data sources, and intelligent systems will be added over time.
